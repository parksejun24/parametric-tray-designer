## Sharp-corner export failure

### Symptom

Valid acute or sharply concave outlines could generate pockets successfully but fail the independent DXF gate with `EXPORT_ROUNDTRIP_CONTAINMENT_FAILED`.

### Root cause

The usable region is created as `offset(P, -m)`, while a final pocket is created through two operations equivalent to `offset(offset(P, -(m-r)), -r)`. Polygon offsets are not associative at acute and reflex corners after coordinate quantization. The second construction could protrude by a few coordinate quanta. The optimizer accepted the tiny area difference, while the independent exporter correctly rejected the protruding edge.

A second verifier-only edge case appeared at star-shaped corners: a parsed DXF endpoint and the corresponding usable-domain vertex could differ by only a few floating-point ulps, yet the segment projection landed infinitesimally outside `[0, 1]` and was rejected before the distance tolerance was applied.

### Change

- `src/core/pocketGeometry.ts`: intersect every round-inset result with the canonical usable domain before topology, area, scoring, and final validation.
- Preserve separate clipped components instead of unioning them, so multi-component topology failures remain visible.
- `src/verification/dxf/verifyExportedDxf.ts`: recognize endpoints within tolerance before projection-range rejection and use a length-scaled projection tolerance.
- `src/core/kernel.ts`: force `unionD` to use the configured four-decimal kernel precision instead of the library's two-decimal default.
- `tests/integration/sharp-outline-export.test.ts`: add acute convex, narrow notch, chevron, needle-reflex, deep V, and high-curvature star regressions, including three-pocket spatial partition and DXF round-trip verification.
- `tests/fixtures/svg/sharp-concave-mm.svg` and `tests/e2e/app.spec.ts`: cover real browser upload, three-pocket generation, validation, and DXF download.
- Updated two golden previews because containment clipping intentionally changes final pocket vertices.
